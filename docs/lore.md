# Lore reference — the real Jimothy & real raccoons

> Research compiled 2026-07-23. This game stars a **real, living animal** and references **real institutions**, so this file separates verified fact from gag from myth. Check here before writing any in-game text, tooltip, or prop.

## Accuracy guardrails (read before writing copy)

- Say **"believed to have short spine syndrome"** — never "diagnosed with." Vets assessed footage only and said his appearance is *consistent with* congenital spine shortening.
- **Do not depict UW conferring an actual degree.** The honorary doctorate was an official-account joke post, not a Regents-conferred degree. A graduation cap prop is fine; a sealed diploma is not.
- **Never depict feeding Jimothy as a good thing.** Public Health — Seattle & King County, WDFW, and a WSU vet all publicly asked people not to feed or approach him (roundworm, leptospirosis). The real story is "leave him wild."
- **Raccoons hoarding shiny objects is a MYTH** with no scientific support (the parallel magpie belief was experimentally debunked). Fine as a joke; never stated as fact.
- Verify the World of Warcraft companion pet actually shipped before referencing it as live.
- Prior art precedent: Chris Pirillo's 8-bit Jimothy game shipped **real raccoon-coexistence tips in its manual** and got warm press for it. A one-screen "real raccoon facts" credits panel is cheap, on-brand, and inoculates the project against "glamourising wildlife harassment."

- **Mariners "Jimothy Night," 5 Aug 2026** — 20,000 rookie cards with chase variants (1-of-1 gold, 1-of-100 silver, 1-of-500 shiny blue, 1-of-2,000 numbered).
- **Bobblehead** (National Bobblehead HoF, benefits PAWS); **Tree House Brewing** put him on a can of *Julius*; **D&D Beyond** stat block: *"Jimothy, Beloved Cryptid,"* Tiny Beast CR 1/4, action **hurl garbage**, flavour *"All who look upon him are blessed."*

## Verified: real raccoon behaviour

- **They are hands with a raccoon attached.** ~**two-thirds** of the sensory cortex is devoted to touch — more than any other studied animal. They identify objects with vibrissae above their claws *before* touching. Five toes per forepaw, **no opposable thumbs**.
- **Dousing** (*lotor* = "washer"): carrying food to water has **never been observed in the wild** — it's a captive fixed action pattern derived from dabbling for shoreline prey. Water **softens the keratinised layer on the paws, increasing tactile sensitivity**.
- **Lock-picking is documented.** Davis (1908): raccoons opened **11 of 13 complex locks in under 10 tries**, and still solved them when locks were **rearranged or inverted**. Memory: **at least three years**.
- **Brains:** cat-sized brain with as many neurons as a dog's, at small-primate density; von Economo neurons present.
- **Toronto lost.** CA$31M of "raccoon-resistant" green bins with gravity locks — raccoons learned to rotate the lock, and that **tipping the bin disengages it**.
- **Dens (WDFW):** tree hollows, rock crevices, burrows, hollow logs, attics, crawl spaces, chimneys, **abandoned vehicles**. They **move den every few days**; >60% of sleeping sites used only once. No hibernation in Washington. Nests lined with leaves, grass, shredded paper, fabric, torn insulation.
- **Latrines:** they establish toilet areas **away from the nest** — the roundworm vector behind the health warnings.
- **Nocturnal but flexible** — coastal WA raccoons forage by day at low tide. The mask likely reduces glare; mask + ringed tail aid reading each other.
- **13 identified calls**, 7 mother-to-kit (not the "50+" that circulates).
- **Lifespan 1.8–3.1 years wild**, ~half of young die in year one. A short-spined kit reaching adulthood in the wild is genuinely against the odds — which is why the story landed.

**Famous individuals:** **Rebecca**, Coolidge's White House raccoon (sent in 1926 to be *eaten*, adopted instead; unscrewed jar lids; got an embroidered collar reading **"White House Raccoon"**). **#MPRraccoon**, 2018 — climbed **25 storeys** of a St. Paul building, caught on the roof with **soft cat food**; the Star Tribune ran a Jimothy-vs-MPR comparison in 2026.
