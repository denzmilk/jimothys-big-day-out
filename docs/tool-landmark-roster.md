# Tools and landmarks

Chris approved tools → landmarks → food progression → tourists → drivers on 2026-10-03. Milestones 43–45 implement the new content before the previously numbered 40–42. Twenty-four tools and sixteen destinations are the working roster; they are not yet claims of implementation.

## Tool roster

| Tool | Distinct use |
| --- | --- |
| Power washer | Narrow sustained water jet pushes props and knocks food from bins. |
| Bubble gun | Temporarily traps a person in a rising bubble, then releases them alive. |
| Leaf blower | Wide air cone sweeps light props away. |
| Vacuum | Directional suction brings loose food to a pile near Jimothy. |
| Food magnet | Short-range all-around food gathering. |
| Fire extinguisher | White spray clears space and propels Jimothy backward. |
| Paint sprayer | Paint splats mark struck people/props and provoke reactions. |
| Confetti cannon | A short colourful burst startles a nearby crowd. |
| Air horn | A narrow sonic blast briefly stuns people. |
| Disco ray | Makes a targeted person dance briefly. |
| Sick ray | Makes a targeted person stop with a cartoon queasy reaction. |
| Stink sprayer | Leaves a lingering cloud that repels people. |
| Spring glove | Close-range spring punch knocks a target away. |
| Suction grappler | Anchors to solid scenery and pulls Jimothy toward it. |
| Plunger launcher | Sticks to a vehicle, briefly slowing it. |
| Tow reel | Pulls one movable prop behind Jimothy, within a size/mass limit. |
| Foam cannon | Deploys temporary physical foam that obstructs a route. |
| Trampoline popper | Places a temporary spring pad that launches a body stepping onto it. |
| Rocket skates | Fuelled forward boost with ordinary collision. |
| Pogo stick | Repeated controlled bouncing and forward motion. |
| Umbrella glider | Slows a fall and allows steering while energy lasts. |
| Bubble shield | Briefly disrupts the animal-control capture attempt. |
| Jackhammer | Repeated local excavation at the feet/front. |
| Firework launcher | Arcing rocket with delayed slapstick blast. |

One slot, physical swap/drop, and a shared energy meter refilled by eating. Fatness and score are retained. Use is suppressed during rolling, capture, pause, fly-camera control and the comet entrance. Size affects useful reach/strength with explicit caps; effects and physics do not grow without bound.

## Landmark roster

| In-game destination | Inspiration | Visit reason / silhouette |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Space Noodle | Space Needle | Crooked tripod observation tower, elevated snack deck and washer cache. |
| Picky Place | Pike Place Market | Long waterfront market hall, fish-sign roofline and food stalls. |
| Frumont Troll | Fremont Troll | Broad crouching stone creature with a trapped car and hidden cache. |
| Gas Guzzlers Park | Gas Works Park | Rusty pipe/tank clusters around picnic lawns. |
| Bandit Locks | Ballard Locks | Paired canal gates, stepped water channel and maintenance tools. |
| Ferry Fiasco | Seattle ferry terminal | Pier, terminal and moored ferry with loading-ramp access. |
| Mono-Rail Yard | Seattle Center Monorail | Elevated beam, station canopy and grounded maintenance depot. |
| Rainforest Bubbles | Seattle Spheres / conservatories | Three varied glass plant domes with shattering panels. |
| Smiff Tower | Smith Tower | Narrow historic tower, stepped crown and pyramid roof. |
| Great Squeal | Seattle waterfront wheel | Large spoke wheel and individual breakaway gondolas. |
| Museum of Loud | Museum of Pop Culture | Asymmetric colourful curved halls and a confetti/disco cache. |
| Hat & Stomps | Georgetown Hat n’ Boots | Huge hat canopy and two distinct oversized boots. |
| Volunteer Waterworks | Volunteer Park water tower | Brick cylinder, arched openings and observation crown. |
| Discovery Light | West Point Lighthouse | Compact lighthouse, keeper’s hut and coastal escape path. |
| Big Pinch Diner | Original edible roadside attraction | Giant red lobster above a seafood diner; later edible in milestone 40. |
| Banana Republic of Snacks | Original edible roadside attraction | Oversized peeled banana shelter and produce stand; later edible in 40. |

Landmark sites reserve space before house generation. Each needs walkable approaches, at least two escape directions, nearby ordinary food and a tool cache. Shape/material/site variation is authored; duplicated copies do not count toward the sixteen. Tourist viewing/parking anchors will be exposed for 41–42. Mount Rainier may remain a distant scenic reference and does not count toward the interactive roster.
